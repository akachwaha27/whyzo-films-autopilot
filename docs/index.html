Whyzo Films

Whyzo Films is a personal video-publishing tool. It turns curious, family-friendly facts and true stories into original ~30 second AI-animated educational videos and uploads them to its owner's own YouTube channel.

What Whyzo Films does

  • Finds popular, non-controversial curiosity topics every few hours from public trend data.
  • Writes an original, fact-checked script and turns it into an AI-animated 3D video with an AI voiceover and captions.
  • Sends the finished video to the owner for review.
  • After approval, uploads the video to the owner's YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.

Why it requests Google access

Whyzo Films asks for two YouTube permissions, used only on the channel of the account that signed in (the owner):

  • youtube.upload: upload the owner's approved videos and set their thumbnails.
  • youtube.force-ssl: add a caption (subtitle) track to those videos and post one comment on them as the owner.

It does not read email, contacts, files, watch history, analytics or any other Google data, and never accesses other users' channels.

Who can use it

Whyzo Films is used only by its owner for their own channel. No account or login is needed to read this page.

YouTube API Services

Whyzo Films uses YouTube API Services to upload the owner's approved videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. By using Whyzo Films you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the app's access at any time at Google security settings.
